About me.

Beyond the Code — The Developer Behind the Work

I’m Muhammad Irfan Waseem, a Computer Science student at COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus. My focus is on mobile development and the backend systems that support it.

I build with Flutter, Dart and Firebase, and use the MERN stack for web applications. Food Fight gave me a way to work through restaurant ordering and fulfillment. MillX ERP brings that same interest in operational workflows to rice procurement, inventory and distribution.

Based in Arifwala and open to remote work, I’m interested in teams that value clear requirements, maintainable architecture and careful testing. I’ve worked independently as a freelance Flutter developer and completed a remote app development internship at Devsphere.
