PORTFOLIO INFORMATION

Privacy & data use.

This page describes the current portfolio's data handling. Last reviewed: November 22, 2026. External services have their own privacy practices.

Contact messages

The contact form requests your name, email address, an optional subject, and a message. Submitting the form sends those fields to Formspree, which processes and delivers them to the site owner. Do not send passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data through this form. You can alternatively use the published email contact link.

Optional public reviews

The review feature uses Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication/Cloud Firestore. When you submit, the current design may store your Google display name, profile image URL, rating, review text, account identifier, consent flag, and submission date. Submissions are not published automatically; the site shows only records explicitly approved for publication and marked with publication permission. Your name, review, rating and eligible photo can be shown publicly only following moderation.

If you want to request removal of a submitted or published review, contact the owner via the contact section. Removal is handled by the owner; this public site does not provide a self-service deletion tool.

Local preferences and third-party services

Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage under portfolio-theme. The site may also request assets or authentication services from Google and Firebase when you interact with review features, and it links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other external websites. Their privacy policies apply once you use their services.

Security and requests

The public portfolio uses browser-side validation and provider integrations. No internet system can guarantee complete security. To ask about your personal data, request correction or deletion, or report a concern, use the email link on the contact page. This notice should be updated if the site's providers or data-handling practices change.
