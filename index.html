InterviewMate AI Pro

Problem

Traditional technical and behavioral interview preparation is fragmented, high-friction, and lacks objective, real-time feedback. Job seekers face high rejection rates due to anxiety, unpreparedness for complex technical probing, and zero actionable guidance on body language and vocal delivery.

My Role

Full-Stack AI Engineer & Lead Architect — conceived, designed, and engineered the complete end-to-end platform, from front-end user experience and FastAPI AI microservices to LLM agent orchestration and computer vision pipelines.

Solution & Architecture

Delivered an autonomous, multi-modal preparation engine featuring a fully functional 6-stage evaluation pipeline:

1MCQ
→
2Written
→
3Technical
→
4HR
→
5Audio
→
6Video
  • Stage 1 (MCQ): Adaptive domain-specific multiple-choice assessments with instant validation.
  • Stage 2 (Written): Coding problem formulation, syntax checking, and algorithmic reasoning tests.
  • Stage 3 (Technical): Deep conversational technical probing powered by real-time LLM agents (Gemini & Groq).
  • Stage 4 (HR Behavioral): Dynamic situational judgment & cultural alignment simulations.
  • Stage 5 (Audio Delivery): Voice transcription, speaking pace, hesitation metrics, and sentiment analysis.
  • Stage 6 (Video & Vision): Computer vision powered by MediaPipe for facial expression, eye contact, and emotion analysis.

Final Result & Outcome

Delivered a production-ready, multi-language AI interview system with support for English, Urdu, and Arabic (RTL). Features comprehensive candidate analytics reports, instant score breakdowns per interview stage, and a full-featured administrative control panel for recruiters and mentors.

Food Fight — Digital Ordering & Management System

Problem

Food Fight, an active fast-food brand, required a unified digital infrastructure to replace manual telephone ordering and costly third-party aggregators. The restaurant needed automated order dispatch, real-time rider tracking, branch inventory management, and multi-tier operational control across management and kitchen staff.

Features

  • 5-Role Architecture: Dedicated workflows for Super Admin, Branch Admin, Kitchen Staff, Delivery Rider, and Customer.
  • Customer Ordering App: Interactive visual menu, meal customization, cart management, instant checkout, and real-time order status tracking.
  • Delivery Rider App: Live order broadcast, one-touch acceptance, and turn-by-turn navigation via Google Maps Platform.
  • Kitchen Staff Portal: Real-time KDS (Kitchen Display System) with instant status transitions (Preparing, Ready for Pickup, Dispatched).
  • Admin & Super-Admin Dashboards: Branch-level analytics, sales reports, inventory stock control, staff assignment, and multi-store oversight.
  • Real-Time Sync & Alerts: Powered by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for low-latency notifications across all five user roles.
