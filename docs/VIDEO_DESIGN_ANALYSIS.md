Sampled-frame review, not full playback or interaction testing. Blank cells represent short clips. No footage, portraits or characters are bundled.

All clips show desktop sites inside portrait recordings, not actual mobile layouts. Exact scroll triggers, cursor velocity, camera easing, animation duration and accessibility cannot be inferred from these samples. Camera changes can include the person filming the laptop. Proposed mobile adaptation: stacked content and static art. Original motion uses short entrance, native scrolling and low-amplitude pointer response. No mandatory intro, continuous particles, autoplay audio or scroll hijacking.
